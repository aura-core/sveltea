# Sveltea Project Summary

This file summarizes the contents and visual direction of the Sveltea website project.

## Website Purpose

The site presents Sveltea as a Spanish-language clinical and editorial landing page for a collagen bioinductor based on calcium hydroxyapatite.

## Visual Direction

- Premium clinical editorial style.
- Light ivory background with black text and intense gold accents.
- Serif display typography with clean sans-serif body text and monospaced clinical labels.
- Mobile-first layout with refined spacing, restrained borders, and clear section hierarchy.

## Main Content

- Hero section with brand positioning, key specifications, and a four-slide media carousel.
- Vertical 9:16 explainer video area for product and procedure animation.
- Explanation of the product mechanism and collagen bioinduction process.
- Product presentation cards for Sveltea and Sveltea+.
- Four-phase mechanism timeline with animated procedure visualization.
- Application tabs for facial rejuvenation and cellulite management.
- Evidence section with clinical references and regulatory notes.
- Professional access area with medical resources.
- FAQ tabs for patients and professionals.
- Contact section with patient and professional routes.
- Regulatory footer with Sveltea, Cellstech, INVIMA, and contact details.
